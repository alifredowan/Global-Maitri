# Global Vivaha Design System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Overview

Global Vivaha is an authentic, mindful Buddhist matrimonial sanctuary designed for lifelong companionship (*Mangala Vivaha*). Grounded in Candidate 7 (*Sacred Auspicious Knot & Royal Heritage Vivaha Scroll / Ashtamangala Patta*), the interface deliberately rejects generic dating app formulas (superficial swipe cards, neon gradients, synthetic metric counters, and SaaS hero templates) in favor of unhurried editorial dignity, natural parchment textures, and sacred geometry.

## Colors (Vaidurya & Serkhang Palette)

- **Vaidurya Lapis Lazuli Indigo (`#17263C`)**: The sacred medicine Buddha & wisdom lapis hue, representing celestial vows, depth of character, clarity, and steadfast dedication. Primary brand anchor.
- **Serkhang Gilded Amber Gold (`#D49B35`)**: Monastic butterlamp gold, embodying auspicious blessings, nobility, warmth, and the eternal knot (*Srivatsa*). Noble accent color.
- **Forest Monastery Jade (`#1E4338`)**: Evokes sacred Bodhi groves, mountain monasteries, and grounded inner tranquility.
- **Lotus Petal Coral (`#C95A70`)**: Used for heart-centered spiritual resonance highlights and the Lotus Privacy Shield.
- **Warm Mineral Chalk (`#F5F3EC`)**: Unbleached Himalayan stone paper / organic mineral ground evoking serenity and timeless manuscripts.
- **Deep Slate Lapis Ink (`#17263C`) & Muted Indigo (`#536277`)**: High-contrast, effortless readability (WCAG AAA compliant on chalk parchment).

## Typography

- **Display & Headings**: `Marcellus`, Georgia, serif. A lapidary Roman face with organic, stone-carved stroke modulation and natural breathing room.
- **Body & Controls**: `Source Sans 3`, system-ui, sans-serif. Highly legible, warm humanist grotesk with clear numerals and punctuation.
- **Measures**: Body prose stays within 65–75 characters per line for optimal reading posture.

## Layout

- **Architectural Harmony**: 8px baseline grid with generous whitespace above sections to foster patience.
- **First Viewport Discipline**: Houses the working Seeker Discovery Vessel directly on the page, allowing immediate filtration by Buddhist Tradition, Country, and Spiritual Practice.
- **No Kicker / Eyebrow Ban**: Headings carry their own narrative weight without superfluous decorative labels.
