# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Buddhist grooms, brides, and serious seekers worldwide seeking lifelong matrimonial companionship (*Mangala Vivaha*). Secondary users are parents and elder family representatives (*Kalyāṇa-Mitta*) participating in biodata reviews and family introductions, as well as elder advisors and monks offering auspicious marriage counsel.

## Product Purpose

Global Vivaha exists to provide a mindful, dignified, and casteless matrimonial space for seekers rooted in Buddhist ethics, loving-kindness (*Metta*), and mutual respect. Success means fostering enduring, harmonious marriages without superficial swiping gamification, predatory recurring subscriptions, or invasive privacy practices.

## Positioning

Unlike conventional dating apps driven by extractive monthly billing and rapid swiping, Global Vivaha offers:
1. **Dhamma-Weighted Compatibility**: Match resonance evaluated on spiritual practice, meditation consistency, adherence to the Five Precepts (*Panca Sila*), and mindful lifestyle.
2. **Family Representative (*Kalyāṇa-Mitta*) Workflow**: A collaborative console for parents and guardians to participate with honor and transparency.
3. **Lotus Privacy Shield**: Server-side photo modesty and controlled bio reveals until mutual interest is established.
4. **Single Marriage Pass**: A one-time payment valid until the confirmed wedding milestone blessing, abolishing recurring subscription traps.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers used by seekers and families across Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar, Nepal, India, Bangladesh, and the global Buddhist diaspora (USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Europe). Used in personal, reflective moments and family discussion circles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Technical Stack**: Laravel 12 on PHP 8.4, Tailwind CSS v4, Blade templates, Laravel Reverb WebSockets for real-time messaging, PostgreSQL / MySQL / SQLite support.
- **Casteless Commitment**: Strictly casteless platform welcoming all Buddhist traditions (Theravada, Mahayana, Vajrayana, Zen/Secular). Dowry demands and caste hierarchies are strictly banned.
- **Verification Guardrails**: ID verification, email confirmation, server-side blur filters, and strict safety scanning on unverified contact exchanges.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Global Vivaha (formerly Global Maitri).
- **Emblem**: Auspicious sacred lotus with entwined eternal knot emblem, rendered in warm, humanized craftsmanship.
- **Tagline**: *Kindness first. Companionship for life.* (Sacred Buddhist Matrimony & Mangala Vivaha).
- **Tone**: Respectful, warm, serene, deeply sincere, and unhurried. Never generic dating app hype or synthetic visual clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

- Fully functional Laravel 12 application with 173 automated tests.
- Comprehensive database schema covering seeker profiles, biodata, family representative linkages, proposals, and verified passes.
- Existing SVG logo elements and layout components in `resources/views/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Loving-Kindness (*Metta*) Over Metrics**: Prioritize intentional companionship over high-frequency engagement traps.
2. **Family Honor & Transparency**: Dignify the role of elder guidance (*Kalyāṇa-Mitta*) while honoring the seeker's personal agency.
3. **Sacred Privacy**: Protect user modesty and personal information until mutual consent is granted.
4. **Authentic Craftsmanship**: Every surface must feel intentional, warm, and crafted—avoiding sterile, interchangeable SaaS templates or AI clichés.
